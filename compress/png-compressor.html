Frequently asked questions

Does this keep transparency?

Yes — the output stays a PNG, so any alpha transparency in your original file is preserved.

Why is my PNG still large after compressing?

PNG uses lossless compression, so savings mainly come from the browser's re-encoding efficiency rather than discarding detail. For much smaller files, consider the JPEG Compressor if you don't need transparency.

Does the quality slider do anything for PNG?

Browsers largely ignore the quality parameter for PNG since it's a lossless format — the slider mainly matters for JPEG and WEBP output.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No, compression happens locally in your browser.
