Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my file get?

It depends on the image and quality chosen — typical savings range from 30-70% at a quality of 60-80%.

Will my photo look different?

At moderate compression (60-85% quality) most people can't tell the difference from the original at normal viewing sizes.

Why compress a JPEG that's already compressed?

JPEGs from cameras or screenshots are often saved at very high quality; re-encoding at a lower quality trims size further for web use.

Is there a limit to how many files I can compress?

No — since it runs locally, use it as many times as you need.
