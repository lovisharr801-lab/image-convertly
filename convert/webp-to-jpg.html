Frequently asked questions

Why convert WEBP to JPG instead of PNG?

JPG produces smaller files than PNG for photographic images, which is useful when file size matters more than pixel-perfect accuracy.

What happens to transparency?

JPG has no transparency support, so any transparent areas in the WEBP are filled with white.

Will there be visible quality loss?

At the default high-quality setting, the difference is generally not noticeable for photos.

Is there a daily usage limit?

No — since conversion runs on your device, you can use it as many times as you like.
