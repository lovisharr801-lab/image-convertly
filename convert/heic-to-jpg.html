About this converter

Turn HEIC photos from an iPhone into widely-compatible JPG files. Chrome, Firefox and Edge have no built-in way to read HEIC files — only Safari does — so this page loads a small HEIC decoder (libheif, compiled to WebAssembly) to do the decoding locally, entirely on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Why won't my HEIC file open elsewhere?

HEIC is Apple's default photo format, and non-Apple software can't display it natively — JPG is supported everywhere.

Why does this take longer than your other converters?

Chrome, Firefox and Edge have no built-in HEIC decoder, so this page loads a small WebAssembly decoder (libheif) the first time you use it. After that first load it's fast.

Does this work in Safari too?

Yes — it works the same way in every browser, including Safari, so results are consistent regardless of what your visitors use.

Is quality lost when converting to JPG?

JPG uses lossy compression, but at high quality settings the difference from the original HEIC is minimal.
