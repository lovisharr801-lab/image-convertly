Frequently asked questions

What is a JFIF file?

JFIF (JPEG File Interchange Format) is essentially a JPEG image, just saved with a .jfif extension — common from some messaging apps and browsers.

Why convert it to PNG?

Some programs don't recognize the .jfif extension even though the image data is JPEG-compatible. Converting to PNG avoids that confusion.

Will quality be affected?

The JFIF/JPEG source is already compressed; re-saving as PNG won't recover lost detail, but it won't lose any further quality either.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No, the conversion is done locally in your browser.
