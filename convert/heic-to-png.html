Frequently asked questions

When should I use PNG instead of JPG?

Choose PNG when you need a lossless copy, or when the image will be edited further and you want to avoid compounding compression artifacts.

Does this work on all browsers?

HEIC decoding support varies by browser. If a file won't load, try Safari, or export as JPG/PNG from your iPhone's share sheet first.

Will the file be larger than the original HEIC?

Usually yes — PNG is lossless and typically produces larger files than HEIC's efficient compression.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No, everything happens locally in your browser.
