Frequently asked questions

Why would I convert WEBP to PNG?

Some older software, design tools, and print workflows don't accept WEBP. PNG is universally supported and keeps transparency intact.

Will I lose image quality?

No — PNG is a lossless format, so the pixel data from your WEBP file is preserved exactly.

Does it keep transparency?

Yes. If your WEBP has an alpha channel, it carries over to the PNG output.

Is this really free?

Yes, with no limit on how many times you use it, since it runs on your own device rather than a paid server.
